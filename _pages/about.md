I am a Ph.D. student in Astronomy at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. My research spans cosmology, dark matter, weak gravitational lensing, baryonic feedback, and physics-informed machine learning. Especially, I focus on interpretable statistical and machine-learning methods that connect astronomical observations, simulations, and cosmology.

Before joining UIUC, I received my B.S. in Physics from the University of Science and Technology of China.
